YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how FITTRACK handles information when you use our fitness tracking website.

1. Information You Provide

When you create an account, FITTRACK collects your email address and account information. You may also enter workout records, body weight, water intake, nutrition logs and fitness goals.

2. How Information Is Used

FITTRACK uses the information you enter to display fitness records, calculate progress summaries and support synchronization between devices.

3. Local and Cloud Storage

FITTRACK uses browser local storage for certain application data. When cloud synchronization is enabled, account data is stored using Supabase. Supabase also provides account authentication.

4. Third-Party Services

FITTRACK uses third-party services to operate the website, including Vercel for hosting and Supabase for authentication and data storage. These providers may process technical information necessary to deliver their services.

5. Advertising

FITTRACK may display advertisements in the future. If Google advertising services are enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure advertisements, subject to applicable consent requirements.

You can learn more about Google's data practices at Google's advertising privacy information .

6. Data Security

FITTRACK uses HTTPS on its hosted website and account-based access controls for cloud-stored fitness records. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

You may choose to use FITTRACK in guest mode or register an account. You can manage the fitness entries you create through available application features.

8. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when FITTRACK features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

ABOUT FITTRACK

Fitness progress, made simple.

FITTRACK is a web-based fitness tracking application designed to help people organize their workouts, record their daily activities and understand their fitness progress over time.

What is FITTRACK?

FITTRACK brings several fitness tracking tools together in one convenient dashboard. Whether you are starting a workout routine or maintaining an established training plan, FITTRACK helps you keep your records organized.

What can you track?

  • Workout sessions, exercises, sets and repetitions
  • Training weights and workout history
  • Daily water intake
  • Body weight changes
  • Food entries, calories and macronutrients
  • Personal fitness goals and progress trends

Our purpose

Our goal is to make fitness tracking accessible and straightforward. FITTRACK focuses on useful tools, clear progress information and an interface that works across modern devices.

Use FITTRACK your way

You can explore FITTRACK in guest mode or create an account to access cloud synchronization. Account holders can use their saved fitness data across supported devices.

Important information

FITTRACK is a fitness logging tool, not a medical service. Information displayed in the application is intended for general fitness tracking and does not replace professional medical or nutritional advice.
